We're rolling out a modernized version of the Club website. It's faster, works well on phones and tablets, and is much easier for Club volunteers to keep current.

Everything from the old site is here — repeater frequencies, the net schedule, meeting information, license testing, and D-STAR details. If you spot anything that's out of date or missing, please let us know at **info@nharc.org**.

On Thursday August 20th, there is an informal gathering at the Hackers Guild to determine how and where to add new antennas.

We have a POTA planned for October 17 at the Pleasant View shelter in Moraine State Park.

The Woods shelter at North Park has been booked for the 2027 ARRL Field Day event.

73 and see you on the air!
