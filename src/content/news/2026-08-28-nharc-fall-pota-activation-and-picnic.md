Saturday, October 17, 2026 at Moraine State Park, South Shore Day Use Area, Pleasant Valley Pavilion No. 2. We have the pavilion from 8am to 8pm (exact start and end times TBD).

Moraine is POTA park US-1387, grid EN90WW. Bring your radio gear and get on the air, or just come for the food. Both are valid choices.

Gary KB3GUZ is bringing and cooking hamburgers and hot dogs, plus potato salad and coleslaw. We need someone to handle coolers, ice, and soft drinks. Everyone else can bring snacks.

Family members are welcome, and so is anyone thinking about joining the club. We need a head count before the picnic, so if you plan to attend, email Gary at pota@nharc.org and let him know how many are coming.

Directions: I-79N to Exit 96 (Portersville/Prospect), right onto PA-488, 1.2 miles to Pleasant Valley Rd, left, then 1.2 miles under RT-422 into the park. Past the park office, go 1.9 miles and turn left into the Pleasant Valley picnic area (3rd left in the park). Pavilion No. 2 is the second one on your left.
