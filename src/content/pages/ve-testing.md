The club holds **Volunteer Examiner (VE) test sessions on the second Saturday of each month**.
Testing begins at **noon**. These sessions are open to anyone seeking a new amateur radio
license or upgrading an existing one, **by reservation only**.

## Where

**Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices — The Preferred Realty (Hampton)**
4100 William Flinn Highway (Route 8), Allison Park, PA 15101

## Fees

- **$15** exam session fee, paid in cash to the VE team at the session.
- **$35** FCC application fee for a **new license**, paid **directly to the FCC** after you
  pass (not collected at the session). Upgrades have no FCC fee.
- If you're under 18, ask the VE team about a special ARRL offer when you reserve.

## Reserve your seat

Reservations are required, and **we do not accept walk-ins**. Please email **ve@nharc.org**
(Bob Worek, AG3U) **at least 24 hours in advance (by midday Friday)**. The examiners drive in
for each session, so if no one has reserved by then, they may not be available.

## What to bring

- **Two forms of identification**, at least one of them a **photo ID** (driver's license,
  passport or state ID). The second can be a birth certificate, Social Security card,
  student ID, library card, or a utility bill, bank statement or postmarked envelope
  showing your current address.
- Your **FCC-issued FRN (Federal Registration Number)**. This is *required*: the FCC no
  longer accepts Social Security numbers, and we can't start your exam without an FRN.
  Register for one on the [FCC CORES website](https://apps.fcc.gov/cores/userLogin.do) ahead
  of time. On your application, use the exact name (including any middle name or initial)
  and the same email address that your FRN is registered under.
- If upgrading: the **original and a photocopy** of your current license and any CSCEs.
  The photocopy is sent in with your paperwork, and without one your upgrade can be
  delayed. There's no copier on site, so make it before you come.
- A **pen** for the forms (pen photocopies more clearly) and a **pencil** for the answer
  sheet.
- A **calculator** if you want one, with no formulas stored in it. Be ready to show the
  examiners that it has none. Phones, tablets and other smart devices can't be used as
  calculators during the exam.
- **$15 in cash** for the exam session fee.

## Minors and special needs

- Candidates under 18 who don't have a photo ID need to bring only one of the IDs listed
  above if a parent or legal guardian presents their own photo ID. Otherwise, bring two
  non-photo IDs.
- If you need any accommodation during the exam, mention it when you reserve your seat so
  the VE team can be prepared.

## On test day

- Arrive a few minutes early. Paperwork and instructions come first, and the session starts
  promptly at noon.
- Silence phones, radios and other devices until everyone has finished testing.
- Only candidates and examiners are allowed in the testing room, so anyone who comes with
  you will need to wait outside it.
- Scratch paper is provided. Please don't write in the test booklet.
- When you finish, the examiners grade your test on the spot. Others may still be testing,
  so please wait quietly.
- If you pass, wait for your **CSCE** (Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination)
  before you leave. It's your proof that you passed.

## After you pass

The VE team sends your paperwork in for processing. Allow up to 10 days for your new or
upgraded license to show up in the FCC's license database.

- **First license:** the FCC emails you a link to pay the $35 application fee, and your
  license is issued once that's paid. You can't transmit until your call sign appears in
  the FCC database.
- **Upgrade:** there's no fee, and you can use your new privileges right away by adding
  **/AG** (General) or **/AE** (Extra) after your call sign until the upgrade shows up in
  the database.

The FCC no longer mails paper licenses; you can download and print your official copy from
the FCC website.
